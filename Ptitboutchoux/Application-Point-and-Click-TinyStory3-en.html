A fantastic and mysterious adventure awaits you in Tiny Story 3! Wake up one morning to find that the sea has disappeared. What happened?  

Solve the mystery and complete all the quests in this fun and challenging point-and-click adventure game with cute graphics and intricate puzzles.

Funny

Many quests and puzzles await you in Tiny Story 3. But that's not all - you can also change your hero's appearance by swapping out hats, scarves, and shoes. Make your hero truly unique as you embark on this exciting adventure.

Simple

Playing Tiny Story 3 is easy - simply touch the objects you want to interact with. Take items, use them together, or keep them in your inventory for later. It's all up to you as you navigate through the game.
