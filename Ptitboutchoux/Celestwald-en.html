 Celestwald Adventure

AppStore   |  Google Play Store   |  Steam  |    itch.io 

Celestwald adventure is an indie point and click game, created with passion by Anne. 

Immerse yourself in the fantastic and humorous adventure of an alchemist apprentice in Celestwald Adventure. Explore the enchanting world and uncover its secrets. Don't forget, Celestwald 2 is also available!

Discover the Grand Master Alchemist Theo and help him harvest the magic seeds of the Celestwald fruit, the tree of dreams. Join the alchemist apprentice on a journey where imagination and logic are essentiel to unraveling the puzzling mysteries of Celestwald.

The stunning artwork, hand-drawn with passion by Anne, and the enchanting music composed by Eric Matyas specifically for the game, bring Celestwald to life.

Enjoy an offline and ad-free adventure and play a gameplay without interruptions.

Puzzles

Solve puzzling quests and enigmas as you embark on an adventure filled with excitement and fun.
