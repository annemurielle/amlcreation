About SonoCalming

A quiet place for long, steady sounds

SonoCalming is a long-form sound project focused on rain, black screen ambience, gentle piano and calm visual environments.

Why I started SonoCalming

SonoCalming grew from my love of steady background sounds and quiet moments. I share rain, nature and carefully selected music for long listening sessions.

The channel grew from that personal need into a library of long rain, dark ambience, noise and gentle music for sleeping, reading, studying or slowing down.

Pure sound. No pressure. Just calm.

What you will find

  • Japanese Bedroom rain, usually 8 to 10 hours
  • Black Screen rain and steady noise
  • Cozy rain and fireplace ambience
  • Relaxing piano and nature soundscapes
  • Occasional watercolor and visual calm projects
