Quick start

  1. Tap + to add a sound (rain, waves, café, white/brown noise, etc.).
  2. Drag each bubble to adjust volume (up/down) and balance (left/right).
  3. Tap the play button to start, set a sleep timer if you wish.
  4. Optional: enable Allow other apps’ audio in Settings to mix with your music app.

Restore purchases

  1. Open Somnifox → Settings.
  2. Tap Restore purchases.
  3. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy Premium.

If you still don’t see Premium unlocked, contact us and include your device model and iOS version.

Troubleshooting

No sound

  • Increase device volume and make sure Silent/Ring switch isn’t muted.
  • In Somnifox Settings, ensure Allow other apps’ audio is on if you’re mixing with music.

Timer stops earlier than expected

  • If Low Power Mode is on, try turning it off.
  • Keep Somnifox in the foreground if your device is very low on battery.

Language

  • Go to Settings → Interface → Language to choose the app language or follow device settings.
