Cats in the box help

Tips: If you hold your finger for more than 1 second on the screen you will see hot points where there is an object or an action to do.

You can use this help in case the game becomes a bit too puzzling for you. If you can not find the answer to your question, you can send it to amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.comand we will reply as soon as possible.

A help system is available directly in the game with the bird Piripili. If it's not enough, you can check the questions below.

Click the questions to view the answers.

Don't forget to save sometimes the game... this will prevent you from losing the game progress (for example: if the game crashed)

Sleepy's adventure

I want to go inside the house.
Have you tried to go into the basement? The door is opened.

I don't understand how to use the buttons.
You need to understand what the drawing on the wall means.
Usually, + means UP and - means DOWN ... but If you interpret the formula on the wall:
+ <> + and - <> -
If + is different from +, that means + becomes -
If - is different from -, that means - becomes +
Result: the buttons must be UP when you have the sign - and DOWN when you have the sign +

I don't understand mathematics and things like that...
You have to understand the relation between the big numbers and the small numbers:
35 and 7 --> 7 X 5 = 35
2 and 0 --> 2 X 0 = 0
11 and 99 --> 11 X 9 = 99
What you need to find is 10 and ? --> 10 X ? = ?
Try to figure out how you can find the multiplicator number.

I don't understand the stars' puzzle.
For this riddle you need to do several actions:
Take the broom in your inventory
Then touch the green picture, direction arrows will appear around it.
Then move the stars: touch a star to activate it, touch a direction image to move it.
Stars can only move on an empty place.
The correct order is: top left: 1 star, bottom left: 2 stars, top right: 4 stars, bottom right: 3 stars
