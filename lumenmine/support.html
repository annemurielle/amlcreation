Help with Lumen Mine

How do I guide the light?

Tap a tile to reveal it, or press and hold to flag it. GO moves the light forward. GO END appears when a safe route to the exit has been revealed.

How do I restore the full game?

Use Restore Purchases in the game with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. A restored iCloud save does not unlock the full game.

How do I transfer progress?

On your old device, open the game settings and save to iCloud. On the new device, use the same Apple Account and restore from iCloud. Confirm the restore to merge progress and keep your best stars and Perfect clears. Settings stay on each device.

Where are leaderboards and achievements?

Open Leaderboards in the game. On iPhone and iPad they use Game Center. Check that you are signed in to see your scores and achievements.
