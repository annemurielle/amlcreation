Lumen Mine Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Lumen Mine app and this website, published by AML Creation.

On-device data

The game stores your progress, stars, Perfect clears, statistics and settings on your device.

iCloud and Game Center

On Apple devices, iCloud saves are transferred only when you use the save or restore commands. Game Center manages the scores and achievements you submit through that service and may display your profile or scores according to your Apple settings.

Purchases

The App Store processes purchases. Lumen Mine uses the purchase result to unlock the full game. Apple handles payment information.

Website and support

This site uses no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. The host may process technical request data to serve pages. If you email support, AML Creation uses the address and message to respond and investigate the issue.

Your choices

You can manage Game Center and iCloud in your Apple settings and contact AML Creation with any privacy question.
