LUMEN MINE

A minesweeper puzzle with a path to find.

Read the numbers, flag hidden mines and reveal a safe route to the exit. You prepare the path. The light follows it.

01

Every tile changes the route.

Reveal a tile to read its clue. Use flags to mark mines, then press GO to move the light. GO END takes it to the exit once a safe route has been revealed.

02

One key, two doors.

Break bricks with limited hammers, unlock doors, activate levers and travel through portals. Spend a key on one door and another may stay locked.

03

Go for a Perfect clear.

You can reach the exit without clearing the grid. Aim for a Perfect when you want to solve more of the board, and earn stars along the way.
