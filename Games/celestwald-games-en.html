The Celestwald collection

One enchanted world.
Two adventures to discover.

From an alchemist’s apprenticeship to a journey with magical seeds, step into the world of Celestwald. Explore hand-drawn scenes, meet forest inhabitants and solve puzzles at your own pace.

Choose your adventure

Game 1 · The Dream Tree

Celestwald Adventure

Become Master Theo’s alchemist apprentice and explore the enchanted forest with Babaorom. Collect useful objects, observe the hand-drawn scenes and solve puzzles to discover the magical seeds of the Dream Tree. The first Celestwald adventure is available on mobile, Steam and itch.io.

iOS · Android · Steam · itch.io

Game 2 · The journey of the seeds

Celestwald 2

Your alchemist’s journey continues: spread Celestwald’s magical seeds across the world. Minimiel accompanies you, while Babaorom offers guidance. Choose your hero and companion, inspect objects and solve puzzles in hand-drawn landscapes. Celestwald 2 is playable offline on iOS and Android.

iOS · Android

Where should I start?

Start with Celestwald Adventure to discover your hero’s apprenticeship and the Dream Tree. Celestwald 2 continues the journey with its magical seeds. Each game has its own page with screenshots, videos and download links.

Need a hint?

Visit the Celestwald Adventure help page for walkthroughs. For Celestwald 2, find the walkthrough videos on its dedicated game page.
