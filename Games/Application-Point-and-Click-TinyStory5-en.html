  Tiny Story 5

Lizzy and the little fairies is an exciting point-and-click adventure game that can be found on both the Appstore and Google Play. The game is available in multiple languages, including English, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Russian, Danish, Portuguese, and Swedish.

 Download on IOS  |   Download on Android


Join Lizzy on her sweet and twisty adventure as she searches for magical ingredients to create a cure for her mom. Along the way, she'll encounter dragons and little fairies and solve many quests. 

Tiny Story 5 features cute graphics and tricky puzzles, with an interface designed specifically for touch devices to ensure a seamless gaming experience.


Funny

If you're looking for a bit of amusement, Lizzy and the little fairies has many quests and puzzles to keep you entertained. Y ou can also change your character's hat, scarf, or dress to add some personal flair.

Simple

The game also has a simple control scheme: just touch the object to pick it up, use it with other items, or keep it in your inventory. And if you're stuck, don't worry – our walkthrough video can help guide you through any tricky spots.

Collect

In addition to the main quests, Lizzy and the little fairies features collectible stamps and stickers that you can find during your adventure. So what are you waiting for? Join Lizzy on her magical journey today!
