Tiny Story 5 – Lizzy and the Little Fairies

Join Lizzy on a magical, heart-warming journey filled with puzzles, cute creatures and charming surprises.

 Download on iOS 🤖 Download on Android 💻 Get it for PC / Mac / Linux

Available in English, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Russian, Danish, Portuguese and Swedish.

A gentle and magical adventure. Lizzy’s mom is sick, and only a special potion can save her. To craft it, Lizzy must explore the forest, meet tiny fairies, encounter a friendly dragon and gather enchanted ingredients.

Every location offers new characters, objects and puzzles to solve. The story is cute, touching and perfect for relaxing moments of exploration.

Tiny Story 5 is fully designed for touch devices: tap to pick up, use or combine items, with no frustration.

A lovely game for anyone who enjoys cozy worlds, soft puzzles and a relaxing pace.

Funny & heart-warming

Enjoy many quests and puzzles. You can also customize Lizzy with different hats, scarves and dresses.

Simple & intuitive

Just tap to interact: take items, combine them, solve puzzles. Accessible and smooth for all ages.

Collect

Find hidden stamps and stickers along the way. A cute bonus for players who love collecting small treasures.
