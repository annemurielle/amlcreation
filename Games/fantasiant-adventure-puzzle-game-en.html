The adventure

A tiny pixel hero is transported into a strange new land. The Hero’s Crystal has shattered, and its unstable magic threatens Fantasiant. Find the scattered shards and help restore balance.

Explore 30 levels across the Green, Fire and Candy worlds. Look for useful objects, solve puzzles and time your actions carefully around dangerous traps. Fantasiant is playable offline without ads, with level help and walkthrough videos when you need guidance.
