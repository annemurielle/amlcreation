Fantasiant

A tiny pixel hero, a shattered crystal, and a land in danger. Collect every shard of the Hero’s Crystal and restore balance to Fantasiant.

You have been summoned as the tiny pink hero to save Fantasiant from the unstable, dangerous magical energy released when the Hero’s Crystal was shattered. Explore, solve puzzles and stay alive in this cute but perilous adventure.

Enjoy a clean and uninterrupted experience: no ads, no online connection required to play.

Gameplay

Collect the crystal shards

Put your puzzle-solving skills to the test and collect all the scattered shards of the Hero’s Crystal. Each level brings new situations, small story moments and playful twists.

Find objects & solve puzzles

Search every corner, find useful items and combine them to advance. Solve funny puzzles, interact with the environment and discover hidden secrets in this quirky point-and-click inspired adventure.

Don’t let danger stop you

You really are a tiny pink hero… but danger is everywhere! Avoid deadly traps, time your moves carefully and use your brain if you want to see the end of your quest.
