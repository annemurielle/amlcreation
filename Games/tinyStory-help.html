Tiny Story 1 – Point & click adventure

Choose your animal hero, explore the tiny town of Tiny and solve puzzles to find your way back home.

 Download on iOS 🤖 Download on Android 💻 PC / Mac / Linux version

A free demo is also available on iOS and Android.

Dive into the charming world of Tiny Story. Wander through the small town of Tiny, talk to quirky characters and collect useful items for your adventure.

Choose your favourite animal hero, explore cosy streets, hidden paths and mysterious rooms, and piece together what happened so you can finally return home.

Everything is designed for touch controls: tap to move, interact, use or combine items. No tiny pixel zones or random tapping, just a smooth and comfortable point & click experience.

Whether you love classic adventure games or are discovering the genre, Tiny Story 1 offers hours of gentle, puzzle-filled relaxation for all ages.

Cute & comforting

Meet adorable characters, enjoy light-hearted dialogues and solve small, low-pressure quests.

Touch-friendly puzzles

Tap to collect, use and combine items. Every interaction is designed for phones and tablets.

Need help? Gentle guidance

If you get stuck, follow the step-by-step walkthrough videos that show each part of the adventure.
