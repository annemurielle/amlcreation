General Tips

  • Hold your finger for one second to display all interactive areas.
  • Remember to save often to avoid losing progress.
  • Some puzzles require combining several items.

Hints & Solutions by Adventure

🐷 Piggy’s Adventure

I can’t unscrew the pipe
 → Find a hidden tool in another room.

The door is blocked
 → Feed the pig — several steps are required.

The mouse doesn’t come out
 → Give it something that smells good.

The house key
 → Hidden box on the outer wall.

🐾 Rocky’s Adventure

Glass door locked
 → Look for another hidden entrance.

How to grab the key
 → Combine two objects and throw them.

😴 Sleepy’s Adventure

Wall formula
 → The + and - signs are reversed.

🎀 Milly’s Adventure

Dirty doll
 → Wash, rinse and dry it using the plugged hairdryer.

😾 Cranky’s Adventure

Wake up the cat
 → Throw objects at the door.

🤪 Crazzy’s Adventure

Yellow door
 → Copy the lever positions from the ceiling.
