Help — Tiny Story 1

Tips, questions & answers, and walkthrough videos for Tiny Story 1. If the game becomes too tricky, this page is here to help you.

For any question that is not listed here, you can write to me at: amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com

General tips

Hint: if you keep your finger on the screen for more than one second, all interactive spots (objects or actions) will be highlighted.

You can use this help page whenever the game becomes a bit too puzzling. If you don’t find the answer to your question, feel free to email me at amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com , and I’ll reply as soon as possible.

You can also find detailed help directly inside the game: tap the Q (Quest) button, then the Detailed Help icon (green question mark). Each scene has its own help, so don’t hesitate to use it.

FAQ & detailed hints

  I can’t access the attic. What should I do?
 The mechanism inside the clock is the key. To open the clock you need to use the two buttons on the stove. The code you have to enter is hidden somewhere.

  I can’t open the door to access the roof. What should I do?
 The code to open the roof is hidden in the bedside table drawer. You must distract the farmer so he drops the key in his pocket, which opens the bedside table.

  I can’t find the purple mushroom. Where is it?
 Take a close look at the outside of the farmer’s house.

  I made a mistake with the magic potion. What can I do?
 The failed brown potion must be used on the witch. The empty bottle can then be reused to brew a new potion. The ingredients you used will reappear where you originally found them.

  I’m stuck in the graveyard.
 Do you have something you can use to dig?

  I can’t figure out how to go further into the tunnel.
 You can’t move forward in the dark. Find something to light the torches in the tunnel. Try searching at the farmer’s place.

  I can’t find the second egg.
 One small egg is in the henhouse, and a big one is on the roof...

  The dog won’t let me pass.
 Find a bone for him to chew on.

  What is that ugly flag with King Rhino?
 Destroy it, it’s the symbol of the Rhinos who invaded the town. Don’t forget to pick up the stick. It might be useful… to throw…

  What should I do in the bar?
 Go to the left of the door. What? There’s a second floor? … Light a fire in the fireplace to reveal some hints…

  What should I do in the bakery?
 Yummy, don’t leave any pastry behind — everything is useful!

  What should I do in the house?
 There are many items to pick up. Don’t miss them! And don’t forget to write down the codes revealed by the lights…

  I can’t enter the police station.
 Have you noted all the pieces of code (two in the house and one in the underground tunnel)?

  How do I enter the museum?
 Find the two stars.

  What should I do in the circus?
 A magic hat without its rabbit isn’t very magical… Have you seen the hidden levers that change how the cannon behaves? But what should you put into the cannon? Maybe something that is in front of the bakery?

  But… there are no stairs to go up to the top floor of the museum!
 Some objects must be put back in the correct place to find the drawer codes… Huh? What’s the connection? You’ll see…

  I can’t open the second drawer in the museum. What can I do?
 There is another way to set the code on the wooden panels. Click once on each lever near the stairs. When you click a lever, the wooden panel that shows the code becomes active (each lever corresponds to one panel). Then you can click directly on the wooden panel to change the code. If you can’t click on the panel, it probably means you clicked more than once on the lever.

  I don’t understand the symbols above the doors in the tunnel. Where can I find a hint?
 You won’t find any direct hint in the game for this code. You have to notice that the symbols are actually numbers (and their mirror). You must add the two numbers at the top and put the result below. Or, if the result is shown, you must find the missing number…

  I don’t understand the code in the last part of the tunnel…
 Did you note the code shown on the TV in the police station? Use this code like this: number 1 means 1 tap, number 2 means 2 taps, and so on.

  I can’t go into the water. I need the fishbowl, right?
 No, you don’t need the fishbowl you saw in the police station. You will find a diving suit if you set the correct symbols near the water. Check the previous point for details about the code.

  What can I give to the fish that wants to eat me?
 This fish loves candy! But you can’t give candy directly to it… So you need to attach one of the candies to something before you feed it to the fish.

  I can’t find the wood to build my boat! Where is it?
 Did you feed the fish? Or is it still hungry? If you already gave it the sugar cane, maybe you can put the small round blue candy somewhere on a rock… and see what happens…

  I can’t use the hammer to build my boat… why?
 You have to combine the hammer with the nails before using it.

  I can’t use the screwdriver to build my boat… why?
 You have to combine the screwdriver with the screws before using it.

  What can I give to the hungry birds?
 Take 3 breads from the bakery…
