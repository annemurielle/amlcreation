Help , Tiny Story 1

Tips, questions & answers, and step-by-step videos for Tiny Story 1. If the game becomes a bit too puzzling, this page is here to help you.

For any question not listed here, you can write to me at: amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com

General tips

Tip: if you keep your finger on the screen for more than one second, you will see the spots where there is an object or an action to perform.

You can use this help page in case the game becomes a little too tricky. If you cannot find the answer to your question, you can send it to me at amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com and I will reply as quickly as possible.

You will also find detailed help directly inside the game: tap the Q (Quest) button, then the Detailed Help icon (the green question mark). Each scene has its own help, so feel free to use it.

Frequently asked questions & tips

I can’t get into the attic. What should I do?
 The mechanism inside the clock is the solution. To open the clock, you need to use the two buttons on the stove. The code you need to enter is hidden somewhere.
I can’t open the door to reach the roof. What should I do?
 The code to open the roof is hidden in the drawer of the bedside table. You will have to distract the farmer so that he drops the key from his pocket. This key opens the bedside table.
I can’t find the purple mushroom. Where is it?
 Look carefully around the outside of the farmer’s house.
I mixed the magic potion wrong. What should I do?
 The failed brown potion has to be used on the witch. The empty bottle can then be used again to make a new potion. The ingredients you used will reappear where you originally found them.
I’m stuck in the cemetery.
 Do you have something you can dig with?
I don’t know how to move forward in the tunnel.
 You can’t move forward in the dark. Find something to light the torches in the tunnel. Look at the farmer’s place.
I can’t find the second egg.
 There is a small egg in the henhouse and a big one on the roof…
The dog won’t let me pass.
 Find a bone for it to chew on.
What is that awful flag with King Rhino on it?
 Destroy it, it’s the symbol of the Rhinos who invaded the town. Don’t forget to pick up the stick. It might be useful… to throw…
What should I do in the bar?
 Go to the left of the door. What? There is a second floor? … Light a fire in the fireplace to reveal some clues…
What should I do in the bakery?
 Yum, don’t forget any sweets, everything is useful!
What should I do in the house?
 There are many objects to pick up. Don’t miss them! … And don’t forget to note down the codes revealed by the lights…
I can’t get into the police station.
 Have you written down all parts of the code (two in the house, one underground)?
How do I get into the museum?
 Find the two stars.
What should I do in the circus?
 A magic hat without its rabbit is not very useful… Have you seen the hidden levers that control the cannon in different ways? But what should you put into the cannon? Maybe something in front of the bakery?
But… there are no stairs to reach the top floor of the museum!
 Some objects must be placed back correctly to reveal the drawer codes… Huh? What’s the link? You’ll see…
I can’t open the second drawer in the museum. What can I do?
 There is another way to set the code on the panels. You need to tap once on each lever near the stairs. When you tap the levers, the wooden panels where the codes appear become active (one lever corresponds to one panel). Then you can tap directly on the wooden panel to change the code. If you cannot tap on a wooden panel, it means you may have tapped more than once on its corresponding lever.
I don’t understand the symbols above the doors in the tunnel. Where can I find a clue for this?
 You will not find any hint for this code inside the game. You need to notice that the symbols are actually numbers (with their mirror). You have to add the two numbers on top and put the result underneath. Or if the result is shown, you must work out the missing number…
I don’t understand the code in the last part of the tunnel…
 Did you write down the code shown on the TV in the police station? Enter this code as follows: number 1 = 1 tap, number 2 = 2 taps, etc.
I can’t go into the water. I need the fish bowl, right?
 No, you don’t need the fish bowl you saw in the police station. You will find a diving suit if you set the correct symbols near the water. Check the previous point for more details on the code.
What should I give to the fish that wants to eat me?
 This fish loves sweets! But you can’t give them directly… so you need to attach one of the sweets to something before giving it to the fish.
I can’t find the wood to build my boat! Where could it be?
 Did you feed the fish? Or is it still hungry? If you already gave it the sugar cane, maybe you can put the small round blue candy somewhere on a rock… and see what happens…
I can’t use the hammer to build my boat… why?
 You must combine the hammer with the nails before you can use it.
I can’t use the screwdriver to build my boat… why?
 You must combine the screwdriver with the screws before you can use it.
What can I give to the hungry birds?
 Take 3 loaves of bread from the bakery…
I built my boat but I can’t leave. Why?
 You need a map before you can leave. Feed the birds to get it.
I’m stuck somewhere else, what can I do?
 Tell us where you are stuck by email at: tinystoryadv@gmail.com . We will answer as quickly as possible.
The game doesn’t work.
 Tell us in which scene the game crashed. Did you try to load a save game you created before? Our address: tinystoryadv@gmail.com . We will answer as quickly as possible.
The game is not available in my language. Can I help translate the game?See the current store listing for the supported languages. If you would like to help translate Tiny Story, contact tinystoryadv@gmail.com for details.
