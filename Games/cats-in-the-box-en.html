The adventure

Piggy, Rocky, Sleepy, Milly, Cranky and Crazzy each have their own little adventure. Discover six stories with distinct kitten personalities, colorful places and puzzles to solve.

Inspect the scenes, collect objects and work out how to use them to help each kitten. Cats in the Box Adventure is playable offline without ads. Interactive help, a dedicated help page and videos for each cat are available when a puzzle needs a little extra thought.

Inside the game

6 kittens, 6 stories
