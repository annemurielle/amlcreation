Meet Puzzles Mix

One picture. Plenty of little discoveries.

Puzzles Mix brings together colorful jigsaw and square puzzles. Take a look at the picture, find how the pieces fit and enjoy the satisfaction of completing the image. It is a simple way to spend a quiet moment with a familiar puzzle format.

Inside the game

Jigsaw puzzles

Find matching edges and put the picture together piece by piece.

Square puzzles

Try another puzzle style with square image pieces.

Colorful pictures

Enjoy a playful collection of images as you work toward a completed puzzle.

Inside Puzzles Mix

Pictures and difficulty levels to explore

Choose from 22 jigsaw puzzles and 132 square puzzles, with three difficulty levels for each puzzle style. Explore cat drawings, flowers and landscape photos, including Brittany, Norway and the Valais. Progress is saved so you can continue later. Play offline without ads.
