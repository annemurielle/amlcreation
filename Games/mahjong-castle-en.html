Meet Mahjong Castle

Match tiles. Follow the story. Discover the castle.

Mahjong Castle brings classic mahjong solitaire into a colorful fantasy adventure. Match identical free tiles, clear the board and follow a story through outdoor worlds and mysterious castle rooms. Discover enchanted eggs, Celestial Beasts and objects for your Collection Books as you progress.

Inside the game

Adventure Mode

Explore 240 Adventure levels across 14 worlds and castle locations, from the Garden and Ice Kingdom to the Library, Tower and Throne Room.

Classic Mode

Complete Adventure level 30 to unlock Classic Mode and play unlimited mahjong layouts at your own pace.

Collect along the way

Discover creatures, eggs, relics, castle objects and ancient seals. Complete collections as the story unfolds.

A helping hand

Use Hint, Shuffle, Undo and Remove Pair when you need support with a board. Choose collectible tile designs to personalize your game.
