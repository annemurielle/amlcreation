Meet Little Minesweeper Guy

A familiar puzzle with a playful face.

Little Minesweeper Guy gives classic Minesweeper a colorful setting. Use the number clues to work out where mines are hidden, mark uncertain squares and decide where to explore next. Each choice is a small logic puzzle.

Inside the game

Read the clues

Use the numbers on the board to reason about nearby mines.

Think ahead

Mark hidden mines and choose the next square carefully.

A cute classic

Enjoy familiar logic gameplay in a warm, colorful presentation.

Inside Little Minesweeper Guy

A puzzle journey across two worlds

Discover 120 levels across two worlds, with special mines that change the challenge. Touch and hold a tile to flag a mine, use helpers when you are stuck and work within each level’s move limit.
