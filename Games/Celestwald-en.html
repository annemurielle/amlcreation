The adventure

Master Theo needs an apprentice to help care for Celestwald, the magical Dream Tree. Choose your hero and enter a fantasy world of unusual creatures, mysterious books and hidden treasures.

Collect objects, help Babaorom and solve the quests and rune puzzles that stand between you and the next discovery. Explore at your own pace with offline play. The detailed help page and chapter walkthroughs remain available whenever you need a clue.
