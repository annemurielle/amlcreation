A warm, relaxing and ad-free story to enjoy at your own pace.

Help a young alchemist apprentice explore a magical forest where every encounter brings a touch of humor, wonder, and mystery.

Assist the Alchemy Master Theo in collecting the magical seeds of the Celestwald fruit, the legendary Dream Tree. Observe, combine items and solve gentle, creative puzzles.

Hand-drawn illustrations and music composed by Eric Matyas bring a unique and soothing atmosphere to the story.
