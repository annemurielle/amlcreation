Tiny Story 4 – Mimy and the Witch of Flowers

Follow Mimy on a magical point-and-click adventure full of puzzles, quests and charming characters.

 Download on iOS 🤖 Download on Android 💻 Get it for PC / Mac / Linux

Available in English, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Russian, Danish, Portuguese and Swedish.

Begin a magical quest with Mimy! After accidentally breaking a precious glassflower, she must find the mysterious Witch of Flowers to repair it.

Explore colorful places, talk to quirky characters and uncover clues that will help you brew the magic potion needed to fix the glassflower.

The game is fully designed for touch devices: tap to collect objects, use items together or interact with characters. Everything is smooth, simple and relaxing.

If you enjoy cute, cozy adventure games, Tiny Story 4 will bring you a heartwarming journey full of puzzles and charm.

Funny & cute

Discover whimsical quests, light-hearted dialogues and dress Mimy with different hats, scarves and dresses along the adventure.

Touch-friendly puzzles

Simply tap objects to pick them up, combine them or use them. Smooth gameplay for phones and tablets.

If you’re stuck, follow the free walkthrough videos to progress at your own pace.
