  Tiny Story 4

Mimy and the Witch of Flowers is an enchanting point-and-click adventure game, available for download on the Appstore and Google Play. Immerse yourself in a beautifully crafted world filled with whimsical characters and intricate puzzles.

The game is available in multiple languages including English, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Russian, Danish, Portuguese, and Swedish, so players from around the world can enjoy the magical journey.

A fantastic and puzzling adventure awaits you in Mimy and the Witch of Flowers! Relax by playing the character of Mimy, who accidentally broke a precious glassflower. Your task is to find the witch who can help you create a magic potion to cure the glassflower. But where is the witch hiding? Join Mimy on her journey to uncover the mystery.

Tiny Story 4 is a fun adventure game with cute graphics and intricate puzzles. With an interface specifically designed for touch devices, you'll navigate the story without incident or frustration.

Funny

Funny and quirky quests and puzzles await you in Mimy and the Witch of Flowers. Not only that, but you can also change Mimy's appearance by trying on different hats, scarves, and dresses.

Simple

Playing Mimy and the Witch of Flowers is easy and intuitive. Simply touch the objects to take them, use them together, or keep them in your inventory.
