The adventure

Mimy has accidentally broken a precious glass flower. The Witch of Flowers might know how to repair it, but she is missing, and a strange curse seems to have reached her manor.

Look for the witch, help a mysterious crying plant and find the ingredients needed for a magical potion. Explore the scenes carefully to collect hidden stickers along the way. Tiny Story 4 is playable offline without ads, with walkthrough videos for its puzzles.

Inside the game

Funny & cute

Discover whimsical quests, light-hearted dialogues and dress Mimy with different hats, scarves and dresses along the adventure.

Touch-friendly puzzles

Simply tap objects to pick them up, combine them or use them. Smooth gameplay for phones and tablets.

Need help?

If you’re stuck, follow the free walkthrough videos to progress at your own pace.
