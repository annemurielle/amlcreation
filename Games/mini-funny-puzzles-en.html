Mini Funny Puzzles

A soft felt world of simple puzzles for little hands.

Mini Funny Puzzles is a children’s game with a cheerful new felt-style redesign. Match shapes, drag the key to the box, explore memory cards and discover friendly scenes such as Ocean Friends, Little Animals and Fairy Garden.

Meet Mini Funny Puzzles

New design coming soon

The current App Store listing still uses the previous visual design. The refreshed felt version shown here is being prepared now and will be published on the same App Store link.

Simple puzzle play in a colorful handmade world

The new art direction turns the game into a soft felt toy box filled with bright colors, friendly faces and clear shapes. The goal is to keep interactions easy to understand for children while making the scenes more playful and more polished.

Inside the game

Shape-matching scenes

Place each felt character on the matching outline and complete the scene step by step.

Little worlds to explore

Enjoy playful themes such as Ocean Friends, Little Animals, Fairy Garden, Funny Monsters and more.

Memory mini-game

Flip cards and match simple pairs in a calm layout made for young children.

Parents area

In-app purchases and external actions are kept inside the parents area.

Hold-to-open safety gate

To open the parents area, the button must be held for a few seconds before the action starts.

Screens from the new design

For kids, with a clear parents gate

Mini Funny Puzzles is designed for children, while the parents area keeps store actions separate. Optional in-app purchases are available there for the full version or extra content, and the gate requires a short hold before opening.

Where to play

Mini Funny Puzzles is currently available on the App Store via the existing listing “Amazing Mini Puzzles Boxes”. The upcoming felt redesign will keep the same App Store link.
