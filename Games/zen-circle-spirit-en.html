Meet Zen Circle Spirit

Follow the glow. Find your rhythm.

Zen Circle Spirit turns a simple tap into a journey through illustrated worlds. Tap at the right moment as the circle moves around the ring, build combos and reveal new backgrounds as you progress. Choose Zen Mode for a gentler pace, or Fast Mode when you feel like a quicker challenge.

Inside the game

One-tap play

A clear rhythm mechanic that is easy to start: watch the moving circle and tap when it reaches the glowing arc.

Evolving worlds

Explore 10 illustrated worlds, including Moon Lotus and Dreamy Cat, with visual progression and unlockable backgrounds.

Choose your pace

Switch between Zen and Fast modes, and try gentle daily quests with simple combo goals.

Keep your discoveries

Explore the background gallery, Game Center achievements and leaderboards as you progress.
