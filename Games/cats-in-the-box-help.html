General Tips

  • Hold your finger for one second to reveal interaction spots.
  • Remember to save often to avoid losing progress.
  • Some puzzles require combining objects together.

Hints & Solutions by Adventure

🐷 Piggy’s Adventure

  • Can’t unscrew the pipe → find a hidden tool.
  • Door is blocked → feed the pig (several steps).
  • The mouse won’t come out → give it something that smells good.
  • The pipe won’t stay → attach it while the water is off.
  • The house key → hidden box on the exterior wall.
  • 4 keys → under a cabbage, near the big tree, in the cabin, and in the forest.

🐾 Rocky’s Adventure

  • Glass door locked → find another way inside.
  • To grab the key → combine two objects and throw them.
  • Buttons become useful only after opening the hatch.
  • 4 codes → washing machine, trash bin, bedroom desk, parents’ bathroom.

😴 Sleepy’s Adventure

  • Wall formula → + and – are inverted.
  • House keys → 1 in the basement, 2 outside.
  • The broom → reach something too high.
  • To go upstairs → solve the baby safety gate math puzzle.

🎀 Milly’s Adventure

  • Glass door → visual clue directly in the scene.
  • Soaps → only one smells good (downstairs).
  • Doll → wash, rinse, dry with a plugged hairdryer.
  • Laundry pipe → exit in the basement (use a support to climb).

😾 Cranky’s Adventure

  • Door won’t open → button is on the other side.
  • Wake the cat → throw objects at the door.
  • Strange box → find keys in the small room.
  • To escape → break the glass door with something falling on it.

🤪 Crazzy’s Adventure

  • The floor “speaks” → someone is underneath.
  • The wood piece → used after Albert’s first mission.
  • Lever room → find coloured pieces.
  • Yellow door → copy lever positions from the ceiling lines.
  • Balloon + box → holes + string + cabbages for weight.
