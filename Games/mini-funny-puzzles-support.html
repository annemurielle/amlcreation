Frequently asked questions

How do I open the parents area?

The parents button must be held for a few seconds before the action starts. This safety gate is used before entering the parents area or triggering external actions.

Are there in-app purchases?

Yes. Optional in-app purchases are available inside the parents area to unlock the full version or additional content, depending on the game build.

Which link should I use?

Please keep using the existing App Store listing for Amazing Mini Puzzles Boxes. The upcoming redesign will stay on that same listing.

How can I contact support?

For help, questions or feedback, please email amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com.

Can progress be saved?

The game is designed to keep a child-friendly experience, and progress or settings may be stored locally on the device, depending on the mini-game and build.

Need more help?

If you have a technical issue, please mention your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Screenshots are also helpful.
