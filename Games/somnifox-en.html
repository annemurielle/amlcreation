Meet Somnifox

Build a soundscape that feels like yours.

Somnifox is the sound app from AML Creation. Layer rain, waves, wind, fireplace, birds, cat purring and background noise into an ambience for reading, focus or quiet moments. Move sounds in space, adjust each layer and save a mix you want to return to. It works offline, without ads or an account.

Inside the game

Layer your sounds

Combine several sound layers and control the volume of each one.

Make the mix your own

Adjust spatial placement, pan, pitch and filter controls to shape your ambience.

Listen your way

Use a sleep timer, keep audio playing in the background or mix alongside music and podcasts.

Free & Premium

Start with one saved ambience, three sound layers and a 30-minute timer. Premium adds more layers, saved ambiences and themes.
