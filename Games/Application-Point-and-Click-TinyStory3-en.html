Tiny Story 3 – Point-and-click adventure

One morning, Wolfy discovers something incredible: the sea has vanished. Explore colorful islands, meet quirky characters and uncover the truth behind this strange event.

 Download on iOS 🤖 Download on Android 💻 PC / Mac / Linux

Tiny Story 3 is a cute and relaxing point-and-click adventure. Travel with Wolfy through small islands, discover clues and help the inhabitants with their everyday problems.

Collect objects, combine items and solve soft, intuitive puzzles. Everything is designed for touch controls and cozy exploration.

The game is available in six languages: English, French, Spanish, Italian, German and Swedish.

If you enjoy gentle mysteries, charming hand-drawn scenery and calm gameplay, Tiny Story 3 is a lovely adventure to dive into.

A charming mystery

Wolfy’s world has changed overnight. Follow clues, talk to locals and piece together what happened to the missing sea.

Smooth touch controls

Touch to explore, collect items and use them together. The interface is simple and comfortable for all ages.
