Meet Lili’s Bedroom

A dreamy bedroom, arranged your way.

Lili’s Bedroom is a colorful room decorating game. Choose furniture, toys, colors and decorative details, then arrange them into a bedroom that feels like your own little world. Explore different combinations and make space for your favorite objects.

Inside the game

Decorate your room

Choose furniture and decorative objects to put your own stamp on a bedroom.

Play with color

Try different colors and combinations for a warm, playful look.

Make it personal

Bring together toys and small details to create your favorite arrangement.

Inside Lili’s Bedroom

Dress up Lili and her teddy bear

Choose hairstyles, outfits, accessories and expressions for Lili, then customize her teddy bear too. Try the shuffle option for a surprising combination. Your choices are retained for your next visit. The game works offline without ads.
