Mini Funny Puzzles privacy policy

Privacy information for the Mini Funny Puzzles app and its support pages.

This page applies to the App Store listing currently published as Amazing Mini Puzzles Boxes, including the upcoming Mini Funny Puzzles redesign shown on this website.

Back to the game pageSupport

Summary

No account required

The game does not require the child to create an account to play.

Local progress and settings

The app may store gameplay progress, preferences or purchase-unlock information locally on the device so the game can work properly.

In-app purchases

If you choose to make a purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. AML Creation does not receive your payment card details.

Support emails

If you contact support, the information you choose to send (such as your email address and the contents of your message) is used only to answer your request.

Children’s use

The game is designed for children. Store-related actions are kept behind a parents area that requires a short hold before opening.

Contact

If you have any privacy question, please contact amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com.
