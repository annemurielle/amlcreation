Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how AML Creation handles personal data in its apps and related services.

1. Introduction

AML Creation develops mobile and desktop applications and related online content. We take privacy seriously and design our apps so that they can usually be used without creating an account or sharing personal details, unless explicitly stated otherwise for a specific service.

2. Purpose of this policy

The purpose of this privacy policy is to inform users about how any personal data provided to AML Creation is collected, used and protected, and what rights users have regarding that data.

3. Data collection and use

In general, AML Creation apps do not collect personal data during installation or normal use. Our apps do not ask for your name, postal address, phone number or similar identifying details.

The situations in which personal information may be processed are mainly:

  • When you contact us directly (for example by email or social media), we receive the information you choose to send us and your contact address so we can reply.
  • When a store or platform (such as the Apple App Store, Google Play, Steam or YouTube) provides anonymous analytics and sales reports. These reports are aggregated and do not allow us to identify you personally.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep support emails and similar communications only as long as they are needed to respond to your request and maintain a record of recurring issues. If you would like these messages to be deleted, you can contact us and we will remove them where technically possible.

To request deletion of information that you have shared with us, please write to: amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com.

5. Children’s privacy

Some AML Creation apps are suitable for children and families. These apps are designed so that they can be used without entering personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by mistake, please contact us and we will delete it where technically possible.

6. Third-party services

Our apps may include links to external services such as app stores, social networks, video platforms or websites. These services have their own privacy policies, which may differ from this one. We recommend that you read the privacy policy of any third-party service you use.

7. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy was last updated on November 21, 2025. AML Creation may update this document if laws, regulations or our products change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle data, you can contact us at: amlcreation.adventuregames@gmail.com.
